# Gaps & Roadmap

What's missing from v0.1.0 and what we expect to add.

## Hard gaps

- ⚠️ **No Phase 1 build.** Three Tier 1 risks in `risks/register.md` block any real implementation.
- ⚠️ **No identity provider.** Open conflict between §2.2 (out of scope) and NFR-001 (server-side entitlement). Needs a build / buy / borrow decision.
- ⚠️ **No subscription / commercial API.** §16 row 2, blocker.
- ⚠️ **No agreed customer / tenancy model.** Implied by the PRD but unstated.

## Soft gaps

- 🔵 **No prototype.** A clickable prototype with seed data was offered in conversation — useful for IA design review before Phase 1 work.
- 🔵 **No Figma / design source.** The dashboard mockup PNG is a static visual reference. Editable design file pending.
- 🔵 **No decision on §18 open questions.** Eight open questions still open.
- 🔵 **No established working agreement on the §16 risks' status** (between TACbot's reading here and any team-side decision).

## Brand dependencies

- The BEACON logo (`assets/beacon-logo.jpg`) should eventually be promoted to the QUEDERA brand kit at <https://github.com/Quedera/brand>. Tracking in `quedera/brand/reference/gaps-and-roadmap.md`.
