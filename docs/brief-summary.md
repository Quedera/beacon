# Brief summary

The v1.0 PRD's executive summary and conclusion, condensed.

## What BEACON is (§1 + §19)

QUEDERA Beacon evolves from an executive insight product into the **central customer-facing hub for the complete QUEDERA portfolio**.

The hub gives each customer a single, coherent location to:

- discover the products and capabilities they own
- understand subscription status
- access reports and dashboards
- manage product access
- navigate into the right QUEDERA product

Beacon retains its primary brand position, but represents all four products clearly: **Pulse, Duet, Atlas, Beacon**. Product-specific experiences remain distinct, but customers don't need to understand the underlying architecture to find what they need.

## Product vision

> Beacon becomes the customer's **QUEDERA control centre**: one place to understand what they have, what is active, what requires attention, and where to go next.

## Core outcomes (§1.2)

- One authenticated entry point to the QUEDERA portfolio
- Clear view of products, subscriptions, entitlements, and renewal dates
- Central catalogue of dashboards, reports, and scorecards across all QUEDERA products
- Product status, service status, and key actions visible at a glance
- Customer administrators can manage users and product access
- Strong product identity preserved while Beacon becomes the portfolio-level experience
- Scalable foundation for future QUEDERA products **without redesigning the navigation model**

## Summary direction (§19)

Make Beacon the central customer portal for the entire QUEDERA portfolio. Beacon owns the common customer experience — discovery, subscriptions, access, reporting, executive navigation. Pulse, Duet, and Atlas remain authoritative for their specialist workflows and data.

This creates a coherent QUEDERA experience **without weakening the identity or value proposition** of the individual products.
