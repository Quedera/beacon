# Scope

PRD §2 — what's in and what's out for the initial release.

## In scope (§2.1)

- Customer-level Beacon home / dashboard
- Products and product detail pages
- Subscriptions, plans, licences, and entitlement status
- Central Reports & Dashboards library
- Cross-product search and filtering
- Recent, favourite, and recommended content
- Customer and user access context
- Notifications and action-required states
- Deep links into Pulse, Duet, and Atlas
- Beacon's own dashboards, reports, and executive insights
- Role-based visibility and access controls
- Audit-friendly activity and subscription history

## Out of scope for the initial release (§2.2)

- Full billing / payment processing
- Replacing the operational functionality of Pulse, Duet, or Atlas
- **Building a new identity provider** ← flag this; see NFR-001 conflict in `risks/register.md`
- Full CRM functionality
- Customer contract authoring
- Advanced analytics authoring across every product

> Items in §2.2 are explicitly *not* Phase 1 Foundation scope. They may appear in later phases or be addressed by other products.
